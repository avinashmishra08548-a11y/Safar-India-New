Privacy Policy — SafarIndiaCabs

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 | Last Updated: October 25, 2026
Platform: SafarIndiaCabs Mobile App & Website (https://safarindiacabs.com)

Welcome to SafarIndiaCabs ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our cab booking aggregator platform via the SafarIndiaCabs website and mobile application. This policy is compliant with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India), and Google Play Console User Data & Data Safety requirements.

Prominent Location Permission Disclosure (Foreground & Background Location):

• For Passengers (Users): SafarIndiaCabs collects precise and approximate GPS location data in the foreground when the app is open to automatically detect your pickup address, calculate accurate intercity road distance and cab fares, and assign the nearest verified cab.
• For Driver-Partners & Active Trips: Location data may be collected in the foreground and background strictly during active, dispatched trips to enable real-time ride tracking, route navigation, accurate pickup coordination, and emergency passenger safety monitoring. Location tracking ceases immediately once a trip is completed or cancelled.

1. Data We Collect

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, Mobile Phone Number (verified via OTP), and Email Address (optional, for receiving GST tax invoices and booking vouchers).
  • Precise GPS Location Data: Pickup coordinates, drop-off location, intermediate stops, and real-time route location strictly for cab booking, pickup coordination, and live trip tracking.
  • Payment & Billing Information: Online payments (20% booking advance or full fare) are processed securely via RBI-authorized PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Razorpay / UPI Intent such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Debit/Credit Cards). We store only the payment transaction reference ID and payment status; we never store your card numbers, CVV, or UPI PIN on our servers.
  • Device & Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, IP address, browser user-agent, and crash logs to ensure app stability, prevent fraudulent bookings, and enable smooth UPI Intent checkout.
  • Driver KYC Documents (For Chauffeurs Only): Driving License, Commercial Vehicle RC, All India Tourist Permit (AITP), and Insurance certificates for safety verification.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Completing Rides & Cab Dispatch: To process your One-Way, Round-Trip, Local Rental, or Airport transfer booking and dispatch a verified AC cab at your scheduled date and time.
  • Driver Assignment & Coordination: To share your pickup details with the assigned chauffeur and send you driver allotment notifications (Driver Name, Mobile Number, and Vehicle Registration Number) via WhatsApp/SMS.
  • Customer Support & Safety: To provide 24×7 helpline support, resolve trip or billing queries, issue GST tax invoices, and monitor trip safety.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. Your data is shared strictly with:

  • Assigned Chauffeur / Fleet Partner: Only your Name, Phone Number, Pickup/Drop Address, and balance fare collectable are shared with the driver assigned to your ride.
  • Authorized Service Providers: Razorpay (for payment processing) and Ola Maps / OpenStreetMap (for distance calculation and geocoding).
  • Law Enforcement / Regulatory Authorities: Only if mandated under applicable Indian law.

4. Data Protection Standards & Security

All data transmitted between your device and SafarIndiaCabs is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS 1.2+ SSL encryption. Access to booking records is restricted to authorized dispatch staff via PIN-protected administrative controls.

5. User Rights & Data / Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or permanently delete your account and personal data at any time:

  • Online / In-App Deletion Portal: Visit https://safarindiacabs.com/delete-account.html or open the "Delete Account / Data" section inside the SafarIndiaCabs app to delete your account immediately.
  • Email / Phone Request: Send an email to safarindiacabs01@gmail.com or WhatsApp/Call +91-7710080992 with your registered mobile number. All personal data is permanently erased within 7 business days (excluding statutory GST tax records required by Indian law).
