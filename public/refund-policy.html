Cancellation & Refund Policy — SafarIndiaCabs

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 | Platform: SafarIndiaCabs (https://safarindiacabs.com)

1. Cancellation Fees Before & After Driver Dispatch

  • More Than 6 Hours Before Scheduled Pickup (100% Free Cancellation): If you cancel your booking at least 6 hours prior to the scheduled pickup time, zero cancellation fee applies and 100% of your advance payment is refunded.
  • Between 2 to 6 Hours Before Pickup (After Driver Allotment, Before Dispatch): 50% of the 20% advance deposit is retained as vehicle allocation fee and 50% is refunded (or 100% of the advance can be adjusted toward a rescheduled trip within 90 days).
  • Less Than 2 Hours Before Pickup / After Driver Dispatch / Passenger No-Show: Once the chauffeur has been dispatched toward your pickup address, has arrived at the pickup gate, or if the passenger fails to board (No-Show), the 20% advance deposit is non-refundable to compensate the driver for fuel and time.

2. Free Date / Time Rescheduling

Customers may reschedule their travel date or pickup time free of cost up to 4 hours prior to the scheduled pickup by calling or messaging our 24×7 support desk at +91-7710080992.

3. Failed / Canceled Bookings by Platform or Driver

  • If SafarIndiaCabs or the assigned driver cancels a confirmed booking due to vehicle breakdown or unavailability and an alternate cab is not provided, you will receive a 100% full refund of the advance paid.
  • If an online payment is debited via Razorpay/UPI for a failed or duplicate transaction where no booking voucher was confirmed, 100% of the debited amount is refunded automatically.

4. Refund Processing Timelines

  • Initiation Time: Approved refunds are initiated within 24 to 48 hours of cancellation request verification.
  • Settlement to Bank / UPI: Refunds are credited directly back to the original payment source (UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, or NetBanking) within 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank's settlement cycle.
