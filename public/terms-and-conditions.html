1. Platform Scope & Aggregator Role

SafarIndiaCabs operates as a cab booking aggregator connecting passengers with verified, independent commercial taxi operators holding valid All India Tourist Permits (AITP).

2. Booking Rules, Fare Estimation & Surge/Hill Pricing

  • One-Way Trips: Chargeable strictly for the one-way distance (minimum 130 KM billing). Zero return fare and zero return driver charges apply. Includes 1 pickup and 1 drop point.
  • Outstation Round Trips: Billed at a minimum package of 250 KM per calendar day (12:00 AM to 11:59 PM). Extra kilometres beyond the daily quota are charged at the applicable per-KM rate for the selected vehicle category.
  • Local Hourly Packages: Offered as 4 Hrs / 40 KM, 8 Hrs / 80 KM, or 12 Hrs / 120 KM within city limits.
  • Hill Station & Peak/Surge Pricing: Bookings to or from hilly terrains (Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, J&K, Ghats) or high-demand peak/night hours include transparent terrain/peak adjustments shown upfront during fare estimation. Vehicle AC may be switched off during steep uphill climbs to prevent engine overheating.

3. Waiting Charges, Tolls, State Tax & Driver Allowance

  • Waiting Charges: Up to 30 minutes of waiting at pickup is complimentary for One-Way and Airport rides. Beyond 30 minutes, waiting charges of ₹150 per hour apply.
  • Tolls, State Border Tax & Parking: Unless pre-included by selecting the "Include Tolls & State Tax" option at checkout, highway FASTag tolls, state entry taxes, green taxes, and parking fees are payable directly by the passenger as per actual official receipts.
  • Driver Allowance: Included in One-Way fares. For Round Trips, standard driver allowance (₹300/day for Hatchback/Sedan/SUV; ₹400/day for Innova Crysta) and night driving allowance (₹300/night between 10:00 PM and 06:00 AM) apply.

4. Payment Methods

  • Bookings are confirmed upon paying a 20% Advance Deposit (or 100% Full Payment) online via Razorpay (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Cards, or NetBanking).
  • The remaining 80% Balance Payable is paid directly to the assigned driver via Cash or UPI during the trip or at drop-off.

5. Driver & Passenger Code of Conduct

  • Smoking, alcohol consumption, and carrying illegal or hazardous items inside the cab are strictly prohibited.
  • Passengers must treat chauffeurs with respect and adhere to legal RTO seating capacities (4 seats for Hatchback/Sedan, 6 for Ertiga SUV, 6–7 for Innova Crysta).
  • Drivers are required to maintain vehicle cleanliness, arrive before scheduled pickup time, and strictly follow non-smoking/non-drinking safety rules.
